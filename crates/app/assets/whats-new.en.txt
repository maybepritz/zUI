## First launch and interface
- First-time setup wizard: language, permissions, services and autostart.
- System theme and notification settings.

## Zapret and services
- AI service checks, a limited watchdog and automatic strategy selection.
- Optional daily retest and statistics.
- Hosts management with backup, conflict checks and rollback.

## Diagnostics
- GitHub issue report with sensitive log lines sanitized.

## Important
Automatic checks are disabled by default. Enabling strategy testing temporarily interrupts Zapret.
